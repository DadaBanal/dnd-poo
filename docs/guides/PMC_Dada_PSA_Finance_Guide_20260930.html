What Finance is, and what it is not

Everyone with Finance

BizBox is PMC's finance system. It stays the place where money is recorded. Dada PSA reads a copy of what BizBox holds, so the desk can put a purchase order or an invoice next to the ticket or project it paid for.

Agreements

Agreements

Finance: Write to changeRead to see

An agreement is the desk's own record of a deal with a supplier or partner. From it you name the BizBox invoices, purchase orders and products it covers, and the desk adds them up.

1

Click the numbered dots, or use Back and Next.

Make one

  1. Finance › Agreements › New agreement. Give it a name, the other party, a start and an end, and an amount with its currency.
  2. It starts as Draft. Make it active when it is signed.
  3. Cover what it pays for: on its page, choose Invoice, Purchase order or Product and give the BizBox number. Or, from an invoice's page, pick the agreement and press Add to agreement.
  4. Read the totals: invoices and purchase orders covered, with their sums, against the agreed amount.

Linking BizBox records to work

TechniciansService managers

Put the money beside the work: a ticket or a project names the BizBox records it concerns.

  1. On a ticket, open the Finance card, choose the kind (purchase order, product, invoice or agreement) and type the BizBox number, for example PO-26-0415.
  2. On a project, the Finance (BizBox) card does the same; the project page shows each linked record.
  3. On the record's own page, Work linked to it lists every ticket and project that points at it, for the people who may see them.

Linking needs Finance Read and Work on the ticket's board. A restricted board's tickets stay invisible to anyone who is not its member, even on a BizBox record's page.

Changing BizBox

Proposing a change to BizBox (today)

Finance: Write

When a BizBox record is wrong or out of date, propose the change on the desk. It is recorded with who asked and why, and waits. BizBox itself does not change.

RecordFields you can propose to change
Agreement (from BizBox)Status, End date
Purchase orderStatus
InvoiceStatus, Due date
ProductName, Unit cost
  1. Open the record (an invoice, say) and find Changes to BizBox.
  2. Pick the field, type the new value, and say why: what the person applying it in BizBox needs to know.
  3. Press Propose the change. The record now shows 1 change(s) waiting for BizBox.
  4. Someone makes the change in BizBox and marks it done (today that is psaspine bizbox-changes --decide on the host). Or you cancel it.
  5. The next sync brings BizBox's own value into the mirror.
StateMeans
Waiting for BizBoxRecorded; BizBox has not changed.
AppliedDone in BizBox, by a person or (later) by the writer, with BizBox's answer recorded.
RefusedBizBox said no (later, by the writer), with its reason recorded.
CancelledWithdrawn by a person.

What the desk refuses

  • A change with no reason: Say why, so the person who applies it in BizBox knows.
  • The value BizBox already has: That is the value BizBox already has.
  • A second change to the same field while one waits: A change to due date is already pending; cancel it first.
  • A field not in the list above: … is not a field that can be changed on this record.
Changing BizBox

The two-way link with BizBox

Finance: WriteAdministrators

Planned, not built

Today the desk only reads BizBox. The design already has a second direction: an approved change on the desk is offered to BizBox by a writer, and BizBox's answer comes back. The writer is not switched on, and nothing can reach BizBox until it is.

Write path

The record, in BizBox and on the desk

How it is designed

  • Only these fields, ever: the list in the section above. The writer cannot touch anything else.
  • Off by default. The writer is registered by configuration on the host, never by the desk. Until then every change stays pending, so nothing reaches BizBox by accident.
  • BizBox decides. Each pending change is offered one at a time. BizBox's own answer is recorded on the change, as Applied or Refused.
  • The mirror follows BizBox, not the proposal: the next sync shows BizBox's value, so the desk never claims a change BizBox did not make.
  • Every step is audited as a BizBox change, with who proposed, who or what decided, and when.

What switching it on would take

A test against a copy of BizBox before the live oneSQL01Not done

Until every row above is done, treat Changes to BizBox as a to-do list for BizBox, not a way to change it. The desk says so on every record page.

For administrators

How the mirror is kept

AdministratorsSQL01

One command copies BizBox into the mirror. It is fenced so that nothing but finance data can cross, because BizBox also holds patient records.

  1. A map file names the BizBox views and columns to read. Nothing is discovered at run time.
  2. Every name must be a plain identifier: no brackets, quotes, spaces or semicolons, so a map cannot carry SQL.
  3. A name that looks like a patient field is refused: patient, admission, diagnosis, PhilHealth, case number, birth date and the like.
  4. The login must not be the SQL administrator and asks for read-only access; the host's read-only views are SQL01's.
  5. Preview first. psaspine sync-bizbox shows what would change; --apply writes the mirror.

Questions people ask

Can I mark an invoice as paid?
No. BizBox's tables do not say whether an invoice is paid, so the desk shows only BizBox's own status: Draft, Posted or Cancelled.
I changed a value and BizBox still shows the old one.
That is expected today. A proposal waits until someone makes the change in BizBox and marks it done, or until the planned write path is switched on. The next sync then shows BizBox's value.
Why can I not see a ticket linked to this PO?
Links show only to people who may see the ticket or project. A restricted board's tickets are for its members only.
The numbers here differ from BizBox.
Look at Last synced at the top of Finance. The mirror is as of that moment; ask for a sync if it is old.
Can an agreement cover a record from another supplier?
Yes; the desk does not check the party. Cover what the agreement pays for, and use the note to say why.
Who can change agreements?
Anyone whose role gives Finance Write. That list is Dada's decision in Setup › Security roles.
