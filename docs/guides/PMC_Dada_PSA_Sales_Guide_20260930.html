How Sales works

Finance: Read to lookWrite to change

An opportunity is a piece of work Dada IT hopes to do. It moves Lead → Qualified → Proposal sent → Won or Lost. Under it sit one or more quotes.

Each stage sets a default probability, shown above; you may type a different one on the opportunity. Sales uses the Finance level: Read to look, Write to create, edit, quote and move.
