The Dispatch page

DispatchersService managers

Dispatch answers three questions at a glance: what has landed with nobody owning it, who is out on visits today, and who answers each board.

Click the numbered dots, or use Back and Next.

Routing a ticket from the unrouted queue

  1. Read the row: its age and its clocks tell you how long it has waited. The queue ages in plain sight, coloured like the board.
  2. Choose the board that should own it under Route to.
  3. Type an owner if you already know who, or leave it for the board's queue or auto-assign.
  4. Press Route. The ticket moves, its clocks carry on, and the move is on its history.

Mail and portal requests land on the unrouted queue when nothing says which board they belong to, for example a department with no board ticked in Setup › Departments.

The on-call rota

Service managersBoard admins

For each board: who is on call now, who is duty manager now, and the shifts behind it. Any member reads it; editing a shift takes Admin on the board.

  1. Pick the role (On-call or Duty manager), the member, the start and the end.
  2. Press Add shift. At 02:00 the name that matters is whoever is on shift, not whoever the board names.
On-call

Paging

Whoever is on call

A page is an urgent call on one ticket. It repeats every 5 minutes, 3 times, until the person acknowledges it on the ticket. If the on-call does not answer, the duty manager is paged the same way.

Try it: a page at 02:00
  • Each attempt is an email to the person and a card in every Teams channel that carries pages. Neither carries the ticket's summary.
  • A page also ends when someone writes a note on the ticket, stops it, or the ticket closes.
  • Start one with Page the on-call on the ticket, or let a workflow rule page for a P1.

Teams cards

Service managersAdministrators

A workflow rule or a page can post a card to a Teams channel. The card carries the ticket number, board, priority, status, owner, the rule's words and a link to the desk: never the summary, never a note.

A channel is named in Setup › Integrations › Teams channels; its webhook address lives only on the host.
