Visits

A visit

Technicians

A visit is a member, a start and an end on a ticket, with a status and a place.

PartMeans
StatusFirm, or Tentative (Setup › Schedule keeps the list).
WhereOn-site, Remote or In-house.
LeadWhen several go, the one who owns the visit.
DoneThe visit happened.
AcknowledgedSet by the member themselves: they have seen it.

Schedule

TechniciansDispatchers
  • Book a visit from the ticket's Schedule card or the form under the week.
  • Time off, a meeting or an activity goes in the same week so nobody books you then.
  • Only me shows your own row; the board filter narrows the people.
  • Every visit change is a ticket event; every block is audited.
The week

Your calendar, and on your phone

Everyone on the desk
  1. Open My profile from your name.
  2. Press Make a feed address and copy it.
  3. Add it to your phone's calendar as a subscribed calendar. It names tickets and places, never what a ticket says. A new address stops the old one.
